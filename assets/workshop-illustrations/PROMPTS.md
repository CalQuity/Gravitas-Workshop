# Image generation prompts

All three images use the built-in image generation tool. English editable slide
text replaces the illustration skill's default embedded Chinese annotations.

## 01-skills.png

Generate one standalone 16:9 horizontal illustration for a university workshop.
Ian Xiaohei visual DNA: pure white background, minimalist thin black hand-drawn
slightly wobbly pen line art, at least 40% empty white space. A deadpan small
solid-black uneven bean-like creature with white dot eyes and thin legs actively
follows a huge unfolding accordion instruction manual on a simple workbench. Its
thin arms turn one folded page while arranging two small blank paper pieces
exactly according to the manual. This is a physical metaphor for an agent skill:
a reusable procedure that guides work, not new model knowledge. Main subject
occupies about 50% of canvas. Tiny restrained orange line marking the active
folded step, one blue pen mark on the next fold. No text, no title, no labels, no
letters anywhere. No shadows, no gradient, no texture, no cream background, no
shiny UI, no formal infographic, no dense diagram, no cute mascot, no children's
illustration. Original composition, not a toolbox or rubber stamp. Clean absurd
product sketch, serious rather than cute. Save the generated image so it can be
inserted in Google Slides.

## 02-sandbox.png

One standalone 16:9 horizontal Ian Xiaohei-style concept illustration for an
English university workshop. Pure white blank background, thin wobbly black
hand-drawn line art, at least 40% white negative space. One small uneven
solid-black deadpan creature with white dot eyes and thin legs works seriously
at a tiny paper-cutting workbench INSIDE a large simple transparent bell-jar
dome. The creature actively cuts a blank paper shape with scissors, showing
productive execution inside a boundary. A single red stop notch and closed lock
where a thin cable reaches the dome's boundary indicate restricted external
access. Only one concept: a sandbox allows work while limiting access. Main scene
occupies about 50-55% of canvas. Sparse orange mark on the workbench and one blue
outline detail. No text, no labels, no title, no letters, no gradients, no
shadows, no textures, no cream backdrop, no formal diagram or UI panels, no cute
mascot poster, no children's art. Original low-tech physical metaphor, clean
absurd product sketch.

## 03-evidence.png

Generate one 16:9 horizontal Ian Xiaohei-style concept illustration. Pure opaque
white background. Thin black wobbly hand-drawn pen line art, restrained orange
and blue accent, at least 40% empty white space. A solid-black small uneven
deadpan creature with white dot eyes and tiny thin legs actively uses a needle
to sew one orange thread between a large blank claim card on the right and a
small source-page receipt on the left. The creature's arms do the sewing, so it
is central to the metaphor. The source receipt has only a few drawn black
horizontal strokes and a tiny blue page corner, no text. One concept: a claim
remains attached to its evidence. Serious clean absurd product sketch, not cute.
Subject occupies about 50% of canvas. No letters or numbers or titles anywhere,
no labels, no shading, no gradients, no shadows, no paper texture, no dark
background, no commercial vector illustration, no dense infographic, no UI
panels, no children's cartoon. Original composition, no bridge, no toolbox, no
fish.
